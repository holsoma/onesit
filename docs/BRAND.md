# OneSIT brand decisions

## Current direction

OneSIT is an open-source, student-led companion app. Use a compact light interface with charcoal lettering and red accents, taking the supplied SIT Vanguard wordmark as a visual reference.

The three wordmark concepts keep identical lettering and compare these bottom lines:

- Student companion
- SITizen companion
- No bottom text

The owner selected **No bottom text**. Use this wordmark throughout the app. The other SVG concepts remain in `assets/brand/concepts/` for reference.

## Affiliation notice

Use exactly:

> Not affiliated with SIT.

Do not add a separate independence statement to this notice.

## About screen copy

> OneSIT brings campus services, academic dates and student events into one place.
>
> An open-source, student-led app piloted by a member of SIT Vanguard. Its wordmark pays homage to SIT Vanguard.
>
> Not affiliated with SIT.

The member's involvement is a personal contribution. Do not describe OneSIT as an official Vanguard initiative or imply organisational endorsement.

## Wordmark construction

Charcoal `#202024` for One and red `#F0323C` for SIT. These are preview colours, not a claim of exact official brand values. Lettering is outlined in the SVGs so the preview needs no downloaded fonts. Tagline versions use a thin rule and tracked capital letters. The version without a tagline also omits the rule.
