# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SIT students opening a phone browser between classes, on public transport or on campus. They need a useful service quickly, the current academic week and a short list of events. There is no account or onboarding requirement.

## Product purpose

Bring SIT services, academic dates and reviewed student events into one compact, open-source app. Start as a static website on Vercel, then package for Android. Optimise loading and interaction latency before adding features.

## Brand personality

Clear, useful, familiar. Red and charcoal connect the identity to the supplied SIT Vanguard reference. A Vanguard member piloted this student-led initiative. The only affiliation notice is: Not affiliated with SIT.

## Design principles

- Make everyday tasks available on the first screen.
- Keep coming-soon services visible with recognisable icons, grey styling and explicit status.
- Keep school authentication on the destination service.
- Store personal preferences on the device. Review newsletter content before public publication.
- Use UK English and concrete copy. No em or en dashes.

## Anti-references

Avoid a university marketing homepage, large promotional hero, full-screen loaders, decorative scrolling and unreadably faint disabled cards.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, 44-pixel touch targets and native browser scrolling. Design for 390-pixel phones and check down to 320 pixels.
